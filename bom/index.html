Parts and costs

This page lists every part of our braille punch test rig, with prices and links. It also shows our running estimate for the full embosser. Our goal is an embosser that costs less than $200 in parts.

How we count costs

  • Cost per build counts only the part of each pack that one build uses. For example, 16 screws from a kit of 340 count as 16/340 of the kit's price.
  • Buy everything is what someone starting with nothing would spend: whole packs, plus tools.
  • Each price shows where it came from. Order sheet means the price on our team's order sheet. Store page means a price we saw on a store's page on the date shown. Estimate means our guess, to replace with a real price.
  • The embosser designs aren't finished, so their costs are early estimates.

Loading the parts list.

The list comes from parts.csv. To change a price or add a part, edit that file. The instructions explain each column.
