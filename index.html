Punch Point

We are a MedLaunch team at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. We are building a low-cost, portable braille embosser for teachers of blind and low-vision students. These 3D models run in your web browser. You don't need to install anything.

Punch test rig

Watch the rig emboss one dot. Change the paper thickness, die height, die size and stylus tip to see how the dot changes.

Six-solenoid head

Type a word and watch the head emboss it, one braille cell at a time.
Design files, print files and code: github.com/b-47/punch-point. Free to use under the MIT license.
